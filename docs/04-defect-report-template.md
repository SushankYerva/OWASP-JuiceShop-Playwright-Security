## Severity guide

| Severity | Definition |
|---|---|
| Critical | Broad compromise or severe impact requiring immediate action |
| High | Significant control failure or major user/data impact |
| Medium | Material but limited failure with a workaround or constrained impact |
| Low | Minor issue with limited impact on intended use |
